About LifeBlood

A smarter way to connect blood donors with those who need it most.

Our Mission

To ensure no life is lost due to lack of blood availability by building a trusted, easy-to-use platform for donors, recipients, and medical staff.

Our Vision

A world where every person in need of blood can quickly find a compatible donor and receive safe, timely support.

For Donors

Create a donor profile, track your donation history, manage your availability, and get notified when your blood type is needed nearby.

For Recipients

Submit blood requests, search for compatible donors, and receive status updates so you can focus on care, not logistics.

Why Choose LifeBlood?

Safe & Verified

Donor eligibility and request details are reviewed by staff to support safer outcomes.

Fast Matching

Find compatible donors quickly using blood group, city, and availability filters.

Private by Design

Sensitive information is protected with role-based access and secure authentication.
